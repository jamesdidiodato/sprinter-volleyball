Game format and standings logic: Modified

Total Rounds: 6 rounds

Assume 1 court for every 2 teams

Courts are ranked:

Court #1 = Highest level

Court #2 = Middle level

Court #3 = Lowest level (if 3 courts exist)

Continue ranking as needed depending on total teams

Round 1 Setup

All teams are randomly assigned to courts for Round 1.

Each court hosts 1 match (2 teams per court).

Ladder Movement Rules (After Each Round)

For each court:

Court #1 (Top Court)

Winner → Stays on Court #1

Loser → Moves down to Court #2

Court #2 (Middle Court)

Winner → Moves up to Court #1

Loser → Moves down to Court #3

Court #3 (Lowest Court)

Winner → Moves up to Court #2

Loser → Stays on Court #3 (if it is the lowest court)

General Rule

Winning team moves up one court (unless already on Court #1).

Losing team moves down one court (unless already on lowest court).

Lowest court losers remain there.

System must automatically:

Reassign courts after each round

Generate next round matchups based on updated court positions
